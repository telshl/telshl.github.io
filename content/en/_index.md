Shih-Hung Lo studies media institutions, the sustainability of journalism, and the relationship between communication and democracy. His work connects the political economy of communication with media policy, platform governance, fact-checking and Chinese media studies. Recent interests include the implications of generative AI for journalism and public communication.

Alongside his academic work, he has participated in media reform and civic journalism initiatives in Taiwan, including weReport. He is a board member and former board chair of the Taiwan FactCheck Center. His work asks how public policy and independent media can support informed public debate in a changing information environment.

**Academic background:** PhD in Media and Communications, London School of Economics and Political Science (2001); MA and BA in Journalism, National Chengchi University (1994 and 1990).

[University profile (Chinese)](https://telecom.ccu.edu.tw/p/406-1088-11475,r786.php?Lang=zh-tw) · [Taiwan FactCheck Center board (Chinese)](https://tfc-taiwan.org.tw/board-of-directors/)

## Research interests {#research}

- **Journalism and democracy:** the sustainability of journalism, public-service media and media reform.
- **Platforms and public policy:** platform accountability, digital governance and the political economy of communication.
- **Fact-checking and public knowledge:** information disorder, trust and independent verification.
- **Chinese media and comparative communication:** media institutions and journalism across political and cultural contexts.
- **AI and communication:** changes in news production and the responsibilities of media institutions.
